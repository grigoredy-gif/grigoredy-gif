## Hi, I'm Edy 👋

Second-year Electrical Engineering student at the **University of Twente** (Netherlands). I like taking
electronics from an idea to a board that actually gets made: requirements, simulation, schematic, layout,
review, ordering, and then finding out why it doesn't work yet.

### Projects

**[QFH weather-satellite station](https://github.com/grigoredy-gif/qfh-weather-satellite-station-)**
My own 137 MHz QFH antenna (designed in MATLAB, simulated in 4nec2, built and measured with a VNA) and an
FM receiver with **no ICs in the signal path**. I recorded Meteor-M satellite passes with an ADALM-Pluto and
SatDump, and wrote MATLAB tools to find out why they wouldn't decode. Receiver version 2, a 252-part
double-conversion board (137.9 → 12.9 → 1.418 MHz), is ordered.

**[Neural-network processor on FPGA](https://github.com/grigoredy-gif/fpga-nn-processor)**
University team project: I led the processor and instruction-set design. A custom processor in Verilog on a
Spartan-7 that classifies all 10,000 Fashion-MNIST test images at 90.24 %, bit-exact with the reference model.
A streaming multiply-accumulate instruction made it 12× faster.

**[LED-Heart](https://github.com/grigoredy-gif/LED-Heart)**
A 72-LED matrix board with an STM8 microcontroller and a shift register, designed to a €15 budget for two
assembled boards. Ordered.

**Electric-bike controller** *(Segreto E-Bikes NL, not public)*
A replacement main controller for the VanMoof S2: ESP32-S3, 36 V battery input, motor-controller interface,
lights, lock and the original display. First boards ordered.
